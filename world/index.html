    Kid safety rules

    • Stay where a grown-up can see you.
    • Know the name of where you're staying (write it on a card in your pocket).
    • If you're lost: stop, stay put, and ask a worker in uniform or a parent with kids.
    • Never go near water, cliffs or traffic without a grown-up.
