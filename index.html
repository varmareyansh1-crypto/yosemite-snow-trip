    Trip notes

    Places to eat ·

    There is no gas in Yosemite Valley and winter dining is limited. Most families in cabins do breakfast at the cabin, lunch in the Valley, and dinner back at the cabin or a reserved Thanksgiving meal.

    Easy cabin meals

    • Slow-cooker chili or soup started in the morning: dinner is ready when you're back cold.
    • Thermos of hot cocoa or soup for the trail; warm drinks matter more than snacks in the cold.
    • Foil-pack quesadillas or grilled sandwiches: fast, kid-friendly.
    • Store groceries inside the cabin or in the bear locker, never in the car.

    Fun for kids

    Snow bingo

    Tap a square when you spot it. Four in a row wins hot cocoa.

    Kid safety rules (read together)

    • Always stay where a grown-up can see you.
    • If you get lost: hug a tree, stay put, and blow your whistle 3 times.
    • Never walk on frozen rivers or lakes, and stay off wet rocks near waterfalls.
    • Never feed or go near animals, even squirrels or deer.
    • Tell a grown-up right away if your fingers, toes or ears hurt or go numb.
